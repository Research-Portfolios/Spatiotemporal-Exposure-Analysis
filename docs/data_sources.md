# Data sources

No input data are included in this repository. The tables in `results/` are aggregated results computed from these inputs.

| Input | Provider | Citation | Access and licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual NO2 and PM2.5 concentration model | CACES empirical geographic regression | Kim et al. (2020), PLOS ONE 15(2): e0228535 | Free download from the Center for Air, Climate, and Energy Solutions. |
| Four-scale decomposition, census block level, 2000-2015 | Mendeley Data, doi:10.17632/bz8pdbcvf2.3 (Wang, 2022) | Wang et al. (2020), Atmospheric Environment 241: 117470 | Free download from the DOI. |
| Census block population by race and ethnicity, 2010 | IPUMS NHGIS | Manson et al. (2024) | Free registration at nhgis.org; redistribution not permitted without permission. |
| HOLC security-grade polygons | Mapping Inequality, University of Richmond | Nelson et al. (2023) | Free download from the University of Richmond; CC BY-NC. |
| Primary jobs by home block and work block (LODES version 7.5) | U.S. Census Bureau, LEHD | U.S. Census Bureau (2021) | Public domain; free download from the Census Bureau LEHD program. |
| Nonattainment designations | U.S. EPA Green Book | U.S. Environmental Protection Agency | Public domain; United States federal government work. |
| Daily wildfire smoke PM2.5 | Harvard Dataverse, doi:10.7910/DVN/DJVMTV | Childs et al. (2022), Environmental Science and Technology 56(19) | Free download; CC BY-SA 4.0. |

The spatial-scale decomposition of the concentrations is the work of Wang et al. (2020), deposited as Wang (2022); the concentration model is that of Kim et al. (2020).
